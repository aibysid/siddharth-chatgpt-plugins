Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how information is collected, used, and handled when you use ChatGPT plugins developed by Siddharth Chauhan (the "Plugin Developer", "we", "us", or "our").

1. Information Processed by the Plugin

When you interact with our ChatGPT plugins, the following categories of information may be processed:

  • User Inputs & Prompts: Text queries, script requests, production instructions, or prompt parameters sent through your ChatGPT interface.
  • API Technical Logs: Standard HTTP request data such as timestamps, request metadata, and response status information that may be generated by supporting infrastructure.
  • Configuration Data: User-selected parameters or preferences provided during an active plugin session.

2. How Information is Used

We process information solely for the following purposes:

  • To execute the requested plugin functions (e.g., generating video concepts, scripts, storyboards, and prompts).
  • To maintain, monitor, and troubleshoot plugin performance and uptime.
  • To enforce security controls and prevent abuse or malicious activity.

We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal information or data to any third party.

3. Data Retention

We follow a policy of minimal data retention:

  • Session Data: Requests and generated outputs are processed in-memory to deliver responses back to ChatGPT and are not retained on our servers after the request completes.
  • Server Logs: Ephemeral system access logs are retained only as necessary for security auditing and diagnostic purposes (typically no longer than 30 days) before automatic purge.

4. Third-Party Services

AI Video Studio is a skills-based plugin that provides instructions and production workflows inside ChatGPT. It does not operate its own remote API or maintain a separate user account system. If you voluntarily follow a recommendation to use an external service such as Videogen.io, that service receives information according to its own privacy policy and terms. Your use of ChatGPT is also subject to OpenAI's Privacy Policy.

5. User Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your data:

  • The right to request access to any personal data held about you.
  • The right to request deletion or correction of your data.
  • The right to object to or restrict certain data processing activities.

Because our plugins process data on a transient basis without user accounts, we generally do not store persistent personal profiles. To exercise any applicable rights, please contact us using the details below.

6. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to safeguard data against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or disclosure, including TLS encryption for all data in transit between OpenAI servers and our plugin endpoints.

7. Children's Privacy

Our ChatGPT plugins are not directed to children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain legal jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, please notify us so we can take steps to remove such data.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in plugin functionality, legal requirements, or operational practices. Any updates will be posted directly to this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
