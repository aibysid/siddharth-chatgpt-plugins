Frequently Asked Questions

How do I enable plugins in ChatGPT?

Ensure you are logged into ChatGPT with an account that supports plugins or GPT actions. Open the plugin store or custom GPT configuration and select the plugin from the directory or installation link.

What should I do if a plugin request fails?

First, verify your network connection and try resending your prompt in ChatGPT. If the issue persists, check our support page or email support with details of the request and error message.

Are my conversations stored by the plugin?

No. Plugin requests are processed ephemerally to generate the requested output and are not permanently stored on plugin servers. See our Privacy Policy for more details.
